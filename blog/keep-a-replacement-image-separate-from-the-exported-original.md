---
title: "Keep a replacement image separate from the exported original"
date: "2026-10-28"
description: "Identify the intended replacement without losing the review history."
---

Keep a revised image separate from the export it replaces during review. Distinct filenames let the handoff name the intended delivery version.

Record the source identifier and the reason for the replacement in the handoff notes. Describe the actual change, such as a different crop, without implying that other properties were checked. Keep the previous output privately if it is still needed to explain the review.

Update the manifest to point to the intended delivery file. If both versions remain in the delivery folder, label their status clearly so the recipient does not have to choose from timestamps.

Open the final folder from the recipient's view when possible and inspect the replacement. Keep the handoff note with that folder so the decision survives a later download or transfer.
