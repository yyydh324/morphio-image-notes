---
title: "Record omitted images in the export handoff"
date: 2026-10-27
description: "Distinguish intentional omissions from failed exports in an image handoff."
---

Keep a note of omitted images beside an export handoff. A folder with fewer files than its source set needs an explanation of deliberate exclusions so the recipient can recognize a missing output.

Use the source identifier and a short reason for each omission. In a hypothetical handoff, one image might be excluded because the owner has not approved it for sharing. Record that limit without copying private review comments into the delivery folder.

Keep failures separate from intentional omissions. A source that failed to export still needs a technical follow-up, while an excluded image may require a decision before any export is attempted. Do not mark either one as a delivered output.

Compare the note with the files in the handoff before sending it. Retain an internal list when the reasons themselves are private, and give the recipient only the information needed to understand the delivered set. Keep the internal and external versions clearly labeled.
